Download help

Start with the frequently asked questions if a link fails, a download expires, or your browser blocks several photos. Check that the source post or public story still opens in TikTok, then copy its share link again.

If the issue continues, email the supported public link, the error message, when it happened, and your browser and device type. Explain whether you were downloading a video, photo post or story. A screenshot of the error can help; remove private details before sending it.

Privacy and copyright

For questions about submitted links and storage, read the privacy policy. Rights holders can review the copyright reporting instructions to see which details to include in a request.

Security

For a security issue, avoid publishing exploit details in a public issue when they could put users at risk. Use the email above for private security reports.

Do not send passwords, payment details, private identity documents, or other sensitive information unless it is necessary to handle your request.
