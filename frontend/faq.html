Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about downloading available TikTok videos, photos and public stories. Start with the homepage download form.

Do I need an account to use TikVideo?

No. TikVideo does not require registration or a user account to submit a supported TikTok URL.

How do I download a TikTok video, photo post or story?

Open the post or story in TikTok and choose Share, then Copy link. Paste it into the homepage download form and select Get download. If media is available, choose the appropriate download button.

What TikTok links are supported?

The service supports HTTPS links from TikTok and supported TikTok short-link hosts. Douyin links are also accepted by the current service.

Can I download TikTok photos?

Yes. Supported TikTok photo posts can be retrieved and the available images can be downloaded individually.

Is TikVideo affiliated with TikTok?

No. TikVideo is an independent web tool and is not presented as an official TikTok service.

Is every TikTok video or photo guaranteed to download?

No. Availability depends on the source media, network conditions, and external systems used to retrieve the media. The site does not promise that every post will always work.

Can I download TikTok stories?

Yes, supported public TikTok story links can return a download while the story is available. Copy the story share link and paste it into the homepage form. Private or expired stories may not work.

Why does a download link stop working after I wait?

Download links generated by TikVideo expire after five minutes. Submit the original TikTok share link again on the homepage to create fresh links, then save the media promptly. Use the post link, rather than the temporary TikVideo download URL.

What should I do if the Paste button does not work?

Your browser may deny access to the clipboard. Long-press inside the homepage URL field on a phone, or use your browser’s paste command on a computer, then submit the link. You do not need to provide your TikTok login details.

Why are not all photos downloaded at once?

Your browser may request permission for multiple downloads or block them. Allow multiple downloads if you choose, or use the download button on each photo to save the images individually.

What should I do if a request fails or there are too many requests?

Check your internet connection and use a supported HTTPS share link to media that is still public and available. If you see a too-many-requests message, wait a minute before retrying. If the error continues, use the contact page and include the public link and error message.

Are all downloads without a watermark?

TikVideo can download videos without TikTok’s watermark when that version is available. If only a watermarked source is available, the download may retain it. TikVideo does not add its own watermark or remove logos, text or watermarks embedded in the original video or photo.
