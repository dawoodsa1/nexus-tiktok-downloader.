Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. TikVideo does not require registration or a user account to submit a supported URL.

How do I download a video, photo or story?

Copy its share link in TikTok, paste it into TikVideo and select Get download. If media is available, choose a download button.

What links are supported?

Use HTTPS links from tiktok.com, www.tiktok.com, m.tiktok.com, vm.tiktok.com or vt.tiktok.com. Supported Douyin links are also accepted.

Does every link work?

No. Private, deleted or expired content may be unavailable. Downloads also depend on the source platform and network conditions.

Is TikVideo affiliated with TikTok?

No. TikVideo is an independent web tool and is not presented as an official TikTok service.

Can I download TikTok photos?

Yes. Supported TikTok photo posts can be retrieved as individual images when the image sources are available.

Can I download TikTok stories?

Yes, supported public TikTok story links can return a download while the story is available. Copy the story share link and paste it above. Private or expired stories may not work.

Tips for downloading TikTok media

Use the original share link and download promptly: download links expire after a few minutes. If a link expires, submit the TikTok link again. Your browser may ask permission to download several photos; you can also save each image separately.
